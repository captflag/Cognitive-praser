# Finishing and Treatment Standards

Accepted finishing processes: reactive dyeing, garment dyeing, enzyme wash, pre-shrinking
(sanforization), and pigment dyeing. Garment-dyed goods must allow for up to 5% shrinkage
and this allowance must be reflected in the panel dimensions.

Any chemical finish must be OEKO-TEX compliant. Record the finish and its compliance
status on the RFQ so suppliers quote against the correct process.
