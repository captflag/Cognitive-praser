# Labeling and Regulatory Policy

All apparel line items must carry fiber-content and country-of-origin labeling per the
destination market's regulations (for example the US Textile Fiber Products
Identification Act). Care labels must state washing and drying instructions consistent
with the specified finish.

Fiber content stated on the tech-pack must reconcile with the material specified on the
RFQ. Any mismatch between the tech-pack fiber content and the RFQ material is a blocking
issue and must be resolved before dispatch.
