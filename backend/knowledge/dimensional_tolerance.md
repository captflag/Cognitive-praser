# Dimensional Tolerance Policy

All cut-and-sew panel dimensions must specify a tolerance. When a tolerance is not
stated on the drawing or tech-pack, apply the house default of +/-1mm for panels under
500mm and +/-2mm for panels 500mm and larger.

Tolerances tighter than +/-0.5mm require sign-off from the production floor before an RFQ
is dispatched, as they materially affect cutting cost and reject rates.

Dimensions must be recorded in millimetres. Convert any imperial measurements before
drafting the RFQ.
